## Finding

ATT = 2.3 percentage points (SE = 0.8, 95% CI [0.7, 3.9], p = 0.004). Urban districts that adopted the tutoring program experienced a statistically significant increase in daily attendance relative to not-yet-treated districts.

## Context

- Specification: TWFE with district and week FE, clustered at district level
- Event study shows clean zero pre-trends (leads 1-6 all within [-0.3, 0.4])
- Effect appears immediately at lead 0 and is stable through lead 4
- Consistent with the program starting on day one of the semester

## Implication

H01a is confirmed pending falsification. The urban effect is real and precisely estimated. Magnitude (2.3pp on a base of 91.2%) is educationally meaningful — equivalent to roughly one additional student present per classroom of 40.
