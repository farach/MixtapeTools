# Binding Decisions

| ID | Decision | Date | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| D01 | Primary estimator is TWFE with district and week FE | 2026-04-01 | Sufficient pre-periods (6 weeks); no staggered-timing bias concern because all treated units adopt simultaneously |
| D02 | Cluster standard errors at the district level | 2026-04-01 | Treatment is assigned at the district level; within-district serial correlation in attendance |
| D03 | Treatment date = January 13, 2026 (semester start) | 2026-04-01 | Program officially began on first day of spring semester; verified with state education department records |
| D04 | Urban = RUCC 1-3; Rural = RUCC 7-9; Exclude RUCC 4-6 | 2026-04-05 | Clean separation avoids ambiguous suburban districts; mirrors USDA classification |
