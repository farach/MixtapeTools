## Claim

Students in rural districts (RUCC 7-9) that adopted the free tutoring program experienced an attendance increase, identified by staggered rollout timing.

## Courtroom

- **Estimand:** ATT of tutoring program on daily attendance rate (percentage points), rural subgroup
- **Population:** Students in grades 3-8 in rural districts (RUCC 7-9) that adopted in January 2026
- **Variation:** Staggered adoption; early rural adopters vs. not-yet-treated rural districts
- **Mechanism:** Same grant application process; rural districts had lower application rates and longer transportation distances to tutoring sites
- **Falsification:** Same specification in Fall 2025 should yield null
- **Sub-claims:** None — this is a sub-claim of H01

## Kills It

1. Pre-trends not parallel in rural subsample
2. Insufficient power — too few rural early-adopters to detect a meaningful effect (N < 30 districts)
